## Who it is for

This service is for seniors and their families: seniors downsizing after years in one home, seniors transitioning to assisted or independent living, seniors relocating closer to family, and families coordinating a senior move — including adult children coordinating from out of town.

## What it includes

Packing support for senior moves, including fragile items and keepsakes. The free video-walkthrough survey room: film only — never lift, move, open, or climb anything heavy or locked — with a human-made estimate. One flat price agreed in writing before move day.

If a senior move involves a very heavy item — a high-mass asset in the 1,500–3,500 lb range — that item moves under Structural Integrity Relocation (SIR), with floor deflection analysis to L/360, as part of the same flat-rate move. SIR is not claimed for senior moves generally; it applies only where such an asset is actually involved.

## How it works

1. Get My Flat Rate Quote — Short (Quick Quote) or Detailed Project, switching in place with From, To, and What's moving preserved.
2. Add the free video walkthrough if wanted — the survey room covers main living areas, bedroom and personal rooms, and keepsakes and fragile items, one prompt per screen, filmed only.
3. The estimate is human-made. The move is quoted flat in writing before move day.
