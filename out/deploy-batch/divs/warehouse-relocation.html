Who it is for

This service is for warehouse and distribution operations and the buyers who plan their moves: warehouse operators, distribution and 3PL operations, manufacturers, and project managers, contractors, and operations buyers planning a warehouse move, a warehouse load-out, or inbound unloading work.

What it includes

Warehouse and distribution moves, warehouse load-outs and inbound unloads, transload staging, and restacking — carried out by supervised warehouse teams. A warehouse load-out is the loading out of a warehouse; an inbound unload is the unloading of inbound freight on arrival. Work is planned around the site itself: access, floor space, staging, and equipment handling, so the move fits the operation instead of interrupting it.

If a warehouse move involves a very heavy item — a high-mass asset in the 1,500–3,500 lb range — that item moves under Structural Integrity Relocation (SIR), with floor deflection analysis to L/360, as part of the same flat-rate job. SIR is not claimed for warehouse moves generally; it applies only where such an asset is actually involved.

2. The job is scoped around the site — access, floor space, staging, and equipment handling — and quoted flat per job, agreed before dispatch.

3. Supervised warehouse teams carry out the load-out, inbound unload, staging, or restack, with no required contract duration.

Standard Job Pricing

Unload $8.00/CWT, Load $10.00/CWT, APU $12.00/CWT, Residential/Driver Meet $400–$425 per man. Quoted per job, adjustable for scope, equipment, travel, and after-hours agreed before dispatch.
